# Częstochowa scene references

Researched 9 November 2026. This file covers only what the November 2026 pass added or corrected: the sky, the trees, the tram's livery and pantograph, the night lighting of Jasna Góra and the new street life. The landmarks and signs were drawn earlier and were not re-researched or moved. The scene remains a **compressed panorama**, not a street view.

## Design choices, not geographical claims

- The Aleje run east to west and end at the monastery, so the scene treats the monastery side as west: the sun rises over the tenements on the right, stands over the avenue at noon and sets beside the tower; the moon crosses the same way. The exact azimuths are artistic.
- Fair-weather cumulus and green limes place the scene in the summer pilgrimage season.
- The pilgrims use the existing pavement lane. Their cross and sign are kept one row below the shop boards, so both are shorter than the real ones.
- Seated café guests and table candles are ordinary terrace life and were not researched against a specific venue.

## Not verified, or left out

- Whether the 2012 cars still carry their delivery livery in 2026 was not confirmed from a dated photograph; the latest MPK image opened is from 2012.
- Kestrels nesting on the tower are reported only by an old local article ([naszemiasto.pl](https://czestochowa.naszemiasto.pl/gniezdza-sie-na-jasnogorskiej-wiezy-dachach-wiezowcow-w/ar/c8-5526013)), so no birds were drawn.
- Lamps on the tower's cross are described by church sources that could not be opened; the single existing light at the spire top was left as it was.
- The present street lamps of the Aleje (globes on curved arms in the photographs above) differ from the scene's straight T-shaped lamps, which were left unchanged in this pass. Whether the globes have since been replaced was not checked.
- The everyday colour scene of the 2018 floodlighting and its switching hours are not published; the cool white follows the 2019 photograph and the schedule is artistic.
- No on-site visit or November 2026 photograph was available.
